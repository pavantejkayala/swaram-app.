💡 Why We Built Swaram (The Human Mission)

Over 40 million visually impaired citizens in India face significant digital exclusion. Existing screen readers (like TalkBack or NVDA) are mechanical, heavily English-dependent, and fail to explain educational concepts conversational-style. We built Swaram (స్వరం) to give every learner an intelligent, empathetic voice mentor in their native regional language.

🛠️ Key Engineering Innovations

  • Sub-Second Hybrid Voice Pipeline: Custom 600ms silence debouncing paired with in-browser native speech synthesis, achieving ~300ms end-to-end response time.
  • Privacy-Preserving Edge Biometrics: 100% client-side Web Audio API MFCC voiceprint lock ensures biometric voiceprints never leave the learner's device.
  • Accessible PDF Study Coach: Real-time text chunking from university textbooks with audio question-and-answer.
  • WCAG 2.1 AAA Compliance: Designed from scratch with 48px tactile touch targets, dual-tone earcons, and zero screen reliance.

Privacy Promise & Siri-Style Background Operation

• Accessibility First: Swaram operates 100% eyes-free for visually impaired and hands-free learners.
• Background Operation: Just like Apple Siri or Google Assistant, Swaram stays active in the background to respond when you call "Hey Swaram".
• Voice Privacy: Your voice is processed in real time solely to answer your questions and is never recorded or sold.
• Local Contacts & Notes: Your saved contacts and uploaded notes stay securely in your own private session.
• Offline Resilience: If internet disconnects, Swaram automatically switches to local voice and offline safety commands.
